Night Bus

OVERVIEW & PREMISE

"Night Bus" is a short-form retro horror game where players experience an increasingly unsettling sequence of nights waiting at a lonely bus stop. Beginning with mundane tasks like picking up trash or reacting to ominous distant screams, the tension steadily builds toward an intense final-night chase sequence where split-second decisions determine survival.

The project was developed as a complete, playable title published on Itch.io (Link to Page), refined through iterative player feedback and community testing.

TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS & ARCHITECTURE

  • Scriptable Object-Driven Event System:
    Designed a modular, data-driven architecture using Scriptable Objects (NightSO) to manage individual night states, dynamic ambient events, trigger conditions, and scene flow without hardcoding level logic.

  • Robust Event-Driven C# Messaging:
    Implemented decoupled C# events and delegates with dynamic subscribe/unsubscribe patterns to manage state transitions, UI updates, and player interaction cleanly across scenes.

  • Custom First-Person Interaction Framework:
    Integrated and adapted modular first-person mechanics with custom interaction interfaces (IInteract), dynamic player state handling, and environmental collision logic.

  • Retro Aesthetic Pipeline (PSX Shader Kit):
    Utilized retro-inspired shader setups to recreate authentic 90s PlayStation-era visual aesthetics, including vertex jitter, affine texture mapping, low-resolution rendering, and custom atmospheric skybox setups.

PROJECT METRICS & TECH STACK

  • Engine: Unity

  • Aesthetics: PS1 / PSX Low-Poly Retro Horror

  • Platform: PC (Itch.io Release)

  • Development Cycle: ~15 Days (Rapid prototyping, polish, post-launch fixes)

  • Role: Solo Developer
